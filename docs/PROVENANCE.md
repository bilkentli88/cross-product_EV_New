# Provenance and validation boundaries

The supplied GitHub ZIP contained the earlier static/E1–E6/E8 protocols and a later XGB-only comparison also labelled E9. The final article uses a different principal E9 plus E10 and E11. This cleanup preserves the original historical numeric CSV bytes, nests those files under `results/historical`, and supplies the actual final notebooks and original result records under the principal paths.

Principal notebooks are copied byte for byte from the delivered final source files. Their code is self-contained; external E6/E3 notebook loading is unnecessary. `provenance/principal_notebooks.json` records their hashes. Original experiment archives are preserved unchanged in the companion exports bundle and identified in `provenance/source_archives.json`. Original protocols, feature diagnostics and individual fit JSONs are immutable source records and can retain older terminology or runtime paths; the root README governs their current manuscript interpretation.

The original E10 export lacked final summaries and completion. These came from the separate verified-summary archive. Its 300 fit records comprise 120 reused E9 endpoints and 180 new fits; all endpoints must be compared within their own protocol. E11's reference-anchor check failed, so all 210 E11 records were freshly trained. E9 and E11 Real scores must not be interchanged.

The cleanup recomputes saved-record means and designated statistics, verifies packaged per-state metrics and original available checksums, checks source/notebook integrity, and runs historical saved-output scripts. It does not retrain the complete study, inspect every physical label, recreate the original GPU environment, or reconstruct absent original historical checkpoints. The retained E11 CPU/checkpoint audit is prior evidence, not a fresh checkpoint evaluation performed during this cleanup.

ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted with notebooks, debugging, analysis/checking code, documentation and manuscript preparation. Measurements are derived from executable analysis of the recorded dataset and saved model outputs. The original archives record implementation hashes, packages and seeds but do not consistently identify assistant model versions. Model-version details are not invented here. The author remains responsible for reviewing assisted code, source attribution and interpretation.
